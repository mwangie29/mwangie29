# LinkedIn About

I’m a full-stack developer and UI/UX designer who turns early ideas into clear, useful digital products.

My work sits at the intersection of **product thinking, interface design, engineering, and security-minded delivery**. I care about the decisions behind a product as much as the final interface: understanding the problem, shaping the experience, building reliable systems, and improving the result through feedback.

I work across:

- **Product and UX/UI design** — user flows, information architecture, responsive interfaces, design systems, prototypes, and interaction details.
- **Full-stack development** — modern web applications, frontend architecture, APIs, databases, integrations, and deployment.
- **Security-aware engineering** — ethical hacking, vulnerability assessment, OWASP-minded reviews, and safer product delivery.
- **Digital brand experiences** — portfolio sites, visual direction, and interfaces that make complex ideas easier to understand.

Selected work includes financial product systems, creative studio experiences, and digital commerce products for the Kenyan market. I also explore the relationship between technology, AI, industrial systems, and human safety.

My working principle is simple:

> **Design the signal. Engineer the system. Protect the surface.**

I’m open to thoughtful collaborations with founders, startups, small teams, and organizations building useful digital products.

If you have an idea worth making clearer, safer, or more useful, let’s connect.
